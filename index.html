Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Downloader - Salvatudo handles information when you use the application.

Last updated: October 13, 2026 Developer: Salvatudo

1. Overview

Video Downloader - Salvatudo (“Salvatudo”, “the app”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is an Android application designed to help users download publicly accessible videos and images from supported third-party platforms by using links provided or copied by the user.

Salvatudo does not require users to create an account and is not designed to access private accounts, passwords, private media, or authenticated session cookies from third-party services.

2. Information you provide

When you paste, share, or copy a supported public link, the app may process that link as necessary to locate and download the publicly available media requested by you. These links are used for the requested download operation and are not intentionally used by us to create a personal profile about you.

3. Downloaded media

Media downloaded through Salvatudo is intended to be stored locally on your device. We do not intentionally upload or retain copies of your downloaded videos or images on servers controlled by us.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of the app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google configuration, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • app interactions, ad interactions, and usage information;
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertising where permitted. You can manage advertising preferences through your device and Google account settings.

5. Analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

Salvatudo may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app performance and how features are used. Depending on configuration and your consent choices, Firebase Analytics may process information including:

  • app launches, sessions, screen views, and feature interactions;
  • device model, operating system, app version, and language;
  • approximate geographic information;
  • app-instance or advertising identifiers where available;
  • in-app purchase and subscription events.

We use analytics information to improve stability, usability, feature performance, and the overall experience of the app.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

If Salvatudo offers paid plans, subscriptions, or an ad-free version through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google may provide the app with information necessary to confirm purchase status, such as the product purchased, subscription status, transaction status, or purchase-related identifiers.

7. Third-party platforms and services

Salvatudo may interact with third-party websites or platforms solely to retrieve content requested by the user from publicly accessible links. Those third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Salvatudo is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by those platforms unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Information processed through the app may be used to:

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used in the app, such as Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Google Play, according to their respective privacy policies and the app's configuration.

11. Data retention and deletion

Salvatudo does not require a user account and we do not intentionally maintain a developer-operated account database containing downloaded media. Locally downloaded files and local app history remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Data processed by third-party services such as Google may be retained according to those providers' own retention policies. Where applicable, users may manage or delete certain advertising or analytics identifiers through Android or Google account controls.

12. Children's privacy

Salvatudo is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to us. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that requires action, please contact us.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of downloaded content complies with applicable laws, copyright rules, licenses, and the terms of the source platform.
